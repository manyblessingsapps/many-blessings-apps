Privacy Policy for Lucky Drop

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Many Blessings Apps built Lucky Drop as a free, ad-supported game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Lucky Drop.

Information Collection

Lucky Drop does not require users to create an account, provide a name, email address, phone number, or other personal contact information.

Lucky Drop uses Google AdMob to provide advertising, including optional rewarded advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Approximate location derived from information such as an IP address
  • App interactions
  • Diagnostic information
  • Device or other identifiers

How Information Is Used

Information collected through advertising services may be used for purposes including analytics, advertising or marketing, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Third-Party Services

Lucky Drop uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. Google's services may collect and process information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Security

Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Children's Privacy

Lucky Drop is intended for a general audience age 13 and older and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
